## Questions

## Basic

* Retrieve the headline numbers: total films, customers, rentals, payments, total revenue and average payment.
* Calculate the number of payments and total revenue for each month.
* Compare the number of films, average rental rate and total rentals for each film rating.
* Identify the top 10 countries by revenue, along with their number of customers.
* Compare the two stores on city, country, number of customers, payments and revenue.

## Advanced

* Retrieve the top 10 most rented films, along with their category and rating.
* Calculate the number of rentals and total revenue for each film category, ranked from high to low.
* Identify the top 10 customers by total spend, along with their country and number of payments.
* Classify the rentals as On time, Late or Not returned, using `CASE WHEN` and `DATEDIFF`, and calculate the percentage of each.
* Find the films that have no copies in any store's inventory, using `LEFT JOIN` with `IS NULL`.
